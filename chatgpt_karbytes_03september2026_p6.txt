### [node[133] — PUBLIC_DOMAIN](https://karlinaobject.wordpress.com/public_domain/)

This page states the project’s licensing philosophy and defines files, intellectual property, open source, public domain, and hyperlink colors.

It defines a file as a finite named bit sequence that can be copied exactly across substrates if the hardware functions correctly. Code files contain executable instructions in programming languages; all other files—including plain text, images, audio, and video—are categorized as media.

Its definition of “open source” is broader and more idiosyncratic than standard software licensing. It claims permission to copy, modify, distribute, sell, and even claim authorship of modified or unmodified copies. The page then presents a supposed copyright paradox in which Person A releases X into the public domain, Person B copies it as Y, declares Y private, and accuses A of copying.

Legally, that scenario is oversimplified: an unchanged copy of public-domain material generally does not acquire a new copyright merely because another person renamed or possessed it. Only sufficiently original new contributions may be protected. The page is best read as karbytes’s philosophical objection to informational ownership rather than as a precise statement of copyright law.

The page declares the files comprising both main websites—and even the author’s genome—to be public domain immediately upon publication. It also treats Wayback Machine preservation as a method of maximizing accessibility and stabilizing intended final forms.

Its identity discussion says there is presumed to be one organism named Kar Beringer/karbytes that originally owns the sites, then qualifies that claim by invoking possible identical organisms across a multiverse and indefinitely many noumenal counterparts.
