It would be a gross oversimplification to say that I am merely trying to to maximize the frequency, duration, and intensity of the pleasure I experience.

I am not merely trying to avoid experiencing any pain or discomfort given that I know that some degree of discomfort (and even pain in some cases) is necessary for accomplishing my objectives such as building and maintaining physical fitness, finishing a tedious intellectual project, or remaining effective in difficult or emergency-level situations (instead of “collapsing” into a useless panic state or relatively mindless reactive state).

Primarily, I am trying to minimize how much I suffer and, secondarily, I am trying to minimize how much any and all beings other than myself suffer (but the degree to which I prioritize minimizing the suffering of a particular being other than myself varies according to how I personally relate to that being).

I define suffering as the experience of one’s time being wasted rather than put to what one would consider to be adequately good use.
